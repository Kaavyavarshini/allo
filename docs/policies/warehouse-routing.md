# Warehouse Routing Guide

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT for the Allo demo. Replace with your own policies.

## Choosing a warehouse
Prefer the warehouse closest to the delivery address. Chennai Hub (CHN) serves same-day
delivery inside Chennai. Bengaluru Hub (BLR) is the default for orders above 50 units
because it has the largest dock capacity. Hyderabad Hub (HYD) serves Telangana and
Andhra Pradesh.

## Splitting an order across warehouses
Split an order only if no single warehouse can fulfil it. Split across at most two
warehouses, and tell the customer that the parcels may arrive on different days.

## Low stock
A SKU is considered low when fewer than 10 units are available at a warehouse. Low-stock
SKUs should be flagged to the replenishment team the same day.
