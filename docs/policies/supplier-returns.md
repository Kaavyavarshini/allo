# Supplier Returns and Replenishment

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT for the Allo demo. Replace with your own policies.

## Replenishment lead time
Standard supplier lead time is 7 working days. Expedited replenishment is available for
a 12 percent surcharge and arrives in 3 working days. Only the replenishment team may
place purchase orders; operators must not create stock manually.

## Damaged goods
Report damaged units within 48 hours of receipt with photos. Damaged units are
removed from on-hand stock only after the quality team approves the claim.

## Return window
Customer returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery if the product is unopened.
Opened electronics are accepted within 7 days only if defective.
